# Starting point: strong engineer, almost no 3D vocabulary; kiln is not the authority

On 2026-10-07 the learner said they are an experienced software engineer who can build systems and tooling, and that terms such as normals, UVs, PBR and texel density are "almost none" of what they can explain or check. They also said kiln as it stands was "a weak uninformed first attempt" to be kept "in name only".

## Implications

- Start from the data model of an asset and explain it in engineering terms (tables, indices, contracts); skip nothing in the 3D vocabulary.
- Tooling, scripting, file formats and automation need no teaching; lean on them for exercises.
- Lessons must not treat kiln's ADRs or gates as correct. The pipeline design is an outcome of the course, not an input.
