# tree_3

Variant 3 of the broadleaf tree. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

4.3 m wide, 4.3 m deep and 7.8 m tall: taller and narrower. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 10. Of seeds 1 to 12 at this size, 7 pass every shape check (1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10). Seed 10 was picked from four looked at under Bevy (4, 5, 6, 10): four pads on a long leaning trunk. A seed draws whole trees until one fills the bounds without being stretched by more than a fifth; the gate, not the generator, refuses a tree that misses the brief.
