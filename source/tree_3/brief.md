# tree_3

Variant 3 of the broadleaf tree. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

4.3 m wide, 4.3 m deep and 7.8 m tall: taller and narrower. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 5. Of seeds 1 to 12 at this size, the generator keeps a tree for 10 and refuses seeds 3 and 6 with their reasons. Seed 5 was picked from two looked at under Bevy (5, 8): four pads stacked up a tall trunk, the top one 3.3 m across and the lowest two under 2 m; its first tree is the one kept. It measures: fork at 2.9 m (brief 2.0 to 3.5), taper 0.76 (at most 0.9), lean 0.42 m, bark seen 0.03 to 0.05 (at least 0.02), sky 0.28 to 0.39 (0.2 to 0.5).
