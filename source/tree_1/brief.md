# tree_1

Variant 1 of the broadleaf tree. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

4.9 m wide, 4.7 m deep and 7.0 m tall: the plain one, the benchmark's size. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 11. Of seeds 1 to 12 at this size, 11 give a tree that passes every shape check (seed 12 has a piece whose tip the fit blunts past 60 degrees). Seed 11 was picked from four looked at under Bevy (1, 3, 4, 11): five pads, with a fork that shows from the standard view. A seed draws whole trees until one fills the bounds without being stretched by more than a fifth; the gate, not the generator, refuses a tree that misses the brief.
