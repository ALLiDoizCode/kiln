# grass_tuft_2, final: observations

From `sheet.png`, its tiles, the aids and `benchmarks/out/grass_tuft_variants.png`. Numbers not read off a tile are the gate's (`out/reports/` and the L1 log).

1. **Silhouette.** front, right, back: a fan of straight-sided spikes from one point, the tallest in the middle and the outer ones splayed to the sides of the frame; every blade can be counted, and background shows between all of them. top: a star of 20 blades, the widest gap between neighbours 40 degrees (brief: at most 90).
2. **Proportions.** Blades are 0.52 to 0.91 m foot to tip (brief 0.12 to 1.1), mean width 0.03 to 0.05 of that (brief 0.02 to 0.1), leaning 13 to 43 degrees (brief 2 to 65): no blade is nearer upright than 13 degrees. The median blade stands 0.077 of its length off the straight line (brief at least 0.03); in the level tiles the curve is hard to see and blades read as straight.
3. **Facing and grounding.** No front. The rootstock sits on the bottom edge of the frame in the level tiles and at the origin in the top tile, about a fifth of the width across.
4. **Topology.** clay_wire: each blade is two stretches and a tip; 124 triangles (budget 250).
5. **Shading.** material tiles: blades are lit smooth; those seen edge-on are thin dark lines. None is black.
6. **Materials.** The tall middle blades are the lightest yellow-green, the short outer ones darker and bluer; the rootstock is a dark brown spot in the top and three-quarter tiles.
7. **Scale.** scale: the tuft is about 0.5 of the figure's height (brief 0.9 m of 1.8 m).
8. **In the engine.** bevy: the tuft is small in the standard view (the camera frames the figure); colours are a little paler than in Blender. Its shadow is as wide as the tuft.
9. **Values.** bevy value map: thin strokes of two greys; no mass. The benchmark's tuft shows fewer, broader strokes, each with a dark foot and a light tip.
10. **At a glance.** bevy squint: "spiky green tuft"; nearer a yucca or a sedge than a soft clump of grass. The benchmark's reads "tall grass blades".
11. **Differences from the brief.**
   - "Stands nearly upright in the middle" (silhouette 3): the most upright blade leans 13 degrees, well inside the limit of 2 but more splayed than the brief's words. The limit does not ask for any blade to be near upright.
   - 20 blades leave the tuft sparse beside the brief's "ground cover"; the budget allows about 45.
   - Blades are one flat colour each; the benchmark's dark foot and light tip along each blade is what ours lacks most at 0.5 m.
