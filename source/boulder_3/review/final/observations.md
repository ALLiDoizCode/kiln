4. **Topology** (`clay_wire`): about thirteen planes, 126 triangles [400]; `back` shows one plane about half the width of the stone.
5. **Shading.** Each plane one tone under its blotches; no face darker than its neighbours without a lighting reason.
6. **Materials.** One grey, lighter on the cap. With blotches 0.3 m across (they were 0.64 m) every plane carries six or more darker patches in `front`, `top` and `bevy`.
7. **Scale.** `scale`: the stone stands a little above the figure's head [1.9 of 1.8 m].
8. **In the engine.** `--stand 3`: planes, edges and blotches all read. `--stand 0.5`: the picture is one plane in one soft tone, with no edge in it and the blotches too faint to see; the first round's picture was the same.
11. **Differences from the brief.** From 0.5 m the stone is still a blank wall: the blotch strength of 12% does not carry at that distance, whatever the patch size. `back` and `right` read as a wedge with one big slanted plane, which `rounded` would measure and the spec does not ask.
