# dome_bush_2

Variant 2 of the dome bush. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/dome_bush/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

2.2 m wide, 2.0 m deep and 1.5 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches to the shoulder, and as wide as a player's arms held out. The origin is on the ground in the middle of the bounds.
