# blade_plant_1

## Real-world size

1.2 m wide, 1.2 m deep and 0.7 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches above the knee: the plain one. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the rootstock; the rosette reaches a little further one way than the other.
