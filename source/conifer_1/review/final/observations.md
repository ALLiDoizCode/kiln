# conifer_1, final: observations

From `sheet.png` (opened whole; no single tile opened beside it, and the review aids were not made). Numbers in brackets are the gate's own (`out/reports`, the build's log).

1. **Silhouette.** material_front, _right, _back: one trunk, bare for the lowest quarter of the height, then five tiers and a top, each tier a drooping skirt, the lowest two the widest [3.8 and 4.0 m, then 2.9, 2.8 and 2.0, and a top of 1.4 m]; the trunk shows between every pair of tiers as a brown bar about a twentieth of the tree's width. The top is a narrow point, a little left of the trunk's line in material_front. material_top: a round, ragged mass about as wide one way as the other, with no trunk seen: every tier is over the one below. bevy and bevy_back (seen from above the tree's own middle): the tiers do not read as tiers; the tree is one green cone with the trunk showing only below it and in a strip near the top.
2. **Proportions.** material_scale: the tree is about 5.3 figures tall (9.5 m over 1.8 m is 5.3); the lowest foliage hangs to about one figure's height. The crown is 0.75 of the height. Tiers are about evenly spaced in material_front, the gap under the top the largest: the brief asks for unequal spacing and this seed does not show it.
3. **Facing and grounding.** The foot flares into roots on the bottom edge of the frame in every level view; the trunk stands on the origin and the crown is not centred in material_top (a little to the right).
4. **Topology.** clay_wire_front: the trunk is six or seven long flat sides with rings only at its foot, 1.3 m and the lowest whorl; the foliage is dense small triangles everywhere, with no large empty faces.
5. **Shading.** material tiles: pieces differ from their neighbours in two or three greens, lighter on top of each tier and darker under it; no face is black or white.
6. **Materials.** Bark brown with dark streaks along the trunk; foliage a mid green, bluer than `tree_1`'s yellow-green.
7. **Scale.** As 2.
8. **In the engine.** bevy: lighter and flatter than the Blender tiles, the tiers lost in one mass from this height. bevy_trunk: bark fills the middle two thirds of the tile with streaks running up it, light and dark about a hand apart; a green edge of the lowest tier in the top right. bevy_under: the leader runs up the middle and five boughs leave it like spokes, bark against foliage and clearly the lighter-and-browner of the two; round them the undersides of the lowest tier, dark green plates with lighter pieces across them; sky shows only in the bottom left corner. The brief says the tiers are seen one above another like rings up a well: they are not, the lowest tier closes the view.
9. **Values / at a glance.** Not done: no aids were made.
10. **Differences from the brief.** (a) From 1 m under the tree only the lowest tier is seen, not tiers above tiers. (b) Seen from above eye height the tiers merge into one mass. (c) The spacing of this seed's tiers is near even. (d) The plates under the boughs read as flat dark shapes with straight edges in bevy_under, not as foliage.
