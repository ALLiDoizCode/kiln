# lily_pad_1

Variant 1 of the lily pads. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/lily_pad/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

A group a stride across: 1.1 m wide, 0.9 m deep and 0.09 m tall, of 5 to 7 pads, the widest 0.35 to 0.6 m across (a serving tray), with one flower 10 to 18 cm across. The origin is on the water's surface at the middle of the bounds.
