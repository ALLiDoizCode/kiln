# tall_grass_3

Variant 3 of the tall grass. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tall_grass/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

0.8 m wide, 0.75 m deep and 1 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches to the hip. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the rootstock.
