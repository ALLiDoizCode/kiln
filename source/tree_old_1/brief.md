# tree_old_1

An old broadleaf tree: growth stage 1.25. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md` (Growth stages), except what is here.

## Real-world size

6.4 m wide, 6.2 m deep and 8.8 m tall: nearly five players tall, with a trunk about 0.55 m through at breast height. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 4. Of seeds 1 to 5 at this size, given 2 trees each for 1 and 2 and 6 each for 3 to 5, the generator keeps a tree for 4 (its first) and 5 (its fourth); the others were refused mostly for a pad with too few points against the sky from below. Seed 4 was taken because its first tree is the one kept, so it builds quickly. It was not picked from several by eye.
