# dome_bush_3

Variant 3 of the dome bush. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/dome_bush/brief.md`, except what is here.

1.1 m wide, 1.0 m deep and 0.75 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches to the thigh. The origin is on the ground in the middle of the bounds.
