# tree_young_1

A young broadleaf tree: growth stage 0.8. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md` (Growth stages), except what is here.

## Real-world size

4.0 m wide, 3.9 m deep and 5.6 m tall: three players tall, with a crown a player can walk under. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 7, the first of seeds 1 to 7 for which the generator keeps a tree at this size (its 22nd; seeds 1 to 6 were refused, mostly for bark seen in fewer than seven views, with 6 or 8 trees each for 1 to 4 and 40 each for 5 and 6). It was not picked from several by eye.
