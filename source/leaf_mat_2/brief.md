# leaf_mat_2

Variant 2 of the leaf mat. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/leaf_mat/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

1.2 m wide, 1.0 m deep and 0.05 m tall: two thirds of a 1.8 m player's height across, two strides. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the bounds.
