# slab_3, final: what the sheet shows

Read from `sheet.png`, the `bevy` tile's aids (`bevy_aids.png`) and `benchmarks/out/slab_variants.png`. Numbers in brackets are the gate's own, from `out/reports/`.

1. **Silhouette.** Wide and low: reads in `front`, `right` and `back`, where the outline is a flat bar with one raised step on it. Two levels of cap: read in every side view and in `three_quarter`. Polygonal outline: reads in `top` only, where the plates have five and six straight sides of unequal length; from the side views the outline is a bar and the sides cannot be counted. Flat caps ringed by chamfers: read in `three_quarter` and `bevy`, as a band round each cap about a fifth to a quarter of the plate's height wide.
2. **Proportions.** 1.4 x 1.2 x 0.3 m. The dominant plate's cap is at about 0.17 m and the taller one's at the top of the bounds. Seen from above, 0.513 of the outline is within 12 degrees of level [brief: at least 0.5]. 2 pieces of 2.45 and 1.05 m2; 0.157 of all surface is buried [brief: at most 0.3]; they show 2.15 and 0.79 m2, a step of 2.73 [brief: at least 1.3].
3. **Facing and grounding.** The base sits on the bottom edge of the frame in `front`, `right` and `back`; no gap shows under either plate. A slab has no front; the taller plate is the only cue to which way it is turned.
4. **Topology** (clay_wire). 208 triangles [budget 400]. Every plane edge carries one bevel strip; the caps are fans of five to seven triangles, long and thin on the dominant plate (its cap is the largest face and has no interior vertex). Where the plates cross there are no extra edges: each keeps its own, and the dominant plate's cap runs on under the taller plate.
5. **Shading.** No face is darker or lighter than its neighbours without a lighting reason in any material tile; no hard edge shows above the ground [load test: none]. The strips light as rounded edges.
6. **Materials.** One grey. In `top` the caps are a mid grey mottled with patches about 0.3 m across, a little lighter and darker; the chamfers and edges are lighter than the caps [exposed edges 1.19 times open faces]; a dark band about 0.1 to 0.2 m wide follows the join on the lower plate's cap and up the taller plate's foot [joins 0.63 times open faces]. No green anywhere: no growth is asked for.
7. **Scale** (scale tile). The slab is a sixth of the figure's height at its highest [brief: 0.3 m of 1.8 m].
8. **In the engine** (bevy, bevy_back). The same shapes and tones as the Blender tiles. The engine's light adds what the texture does not have: the lit caps are about two values lighter than the walls facing away, and the slab casts a shadow a plate's height wide. The mottling is plainer in Bevy than in Blender's `top` tile: it is the strongest thing on the caps.
9. **Values** (value map of `bevy`). Two masses: the taller plate's lit cap (lightest) and the shadowed walls with the cast shadow (darkest). The dominant plate's cap is the same grey as the ground plane and vanishes into it except for scattered flecks of the mottling. The benchmark rock shows three (lit top, mid sides, mossy base).
10. **At a glance** (squint of `bevy`). "Flat grey stone." The eye lands on the taller plate's shadowed wall, the one strong dark edge. The benchmark reads as "mossy boulder" and the eye lands on its lit top.
11. **Differences from the brief.**
    - top: the taller plate covers about a fifth of the outline and sits at the right rim, half off the dominant plate.
    - The dominant plate's cap has the ground's own value in the review scene (item 9), so the slab reads as one small raised stone on a faint outline. On green or dark ground this may not hold; it was not tried.
    - The mottling (blotch 0.12 over 0.3 m) reads as camouflage patches at 0.5 m (`slab_variants.png`, `--stand 0.5`), not as stone. The boulder's 0.6 m patches were softer; the size was halved to give the smallest slab's cap a measurable spread.
    - Beside the benchmark the slabs are plain: no moss, no second colour. The brief leaves growth out on purpose (covers are palette variants); the owner should see the comparison before that stands.
    - Silhouette 5 (five to seven sides) and 8 (chamfer ring) have no check; both read as described in item 1.
