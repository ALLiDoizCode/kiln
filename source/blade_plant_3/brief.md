# blade_plant_3

Variant 3 of the blade plant. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/blade_plant/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

0.8 m wide, 0.75 m deep and 0.45 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches to the knee. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the rootstock; the rosette reaches a little further one way than the other.
