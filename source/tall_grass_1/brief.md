# tall_grass_1

Variant 1 of the tall grass. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tall_grass/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

1 m wide, 1 m deep and 1.15 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches to the waist. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the rootstock.
