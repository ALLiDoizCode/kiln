# crag_1_mossy, final: observations

Read from `sheet.png` and `benchmarks/out/covers_mossy.png`. The review aids were not made; no second reader. Not approved.

1. **Silhouette, proportions, topology, scale**: as `crag_1`; the gate holds the mesh to `crag_1`'s byte for byte (L2c).
2. **Materials**: an olive wash from the ground to about 0.9 m on every piece, with a soft ragged top; the three foot blocks (all under 0.9 m) are wholly green. Dark olive flecks down the upper edges of the tallest prism and on its cap; the caps of the shorter prisms carry few. Load test: growth on 1.00 of the surface below 0.36 m, 0.02 of upright open faces above 1.44 m, 0.21 of near-level faces, 0.46 of upper edges; patches 0.50 darker.
3. **In the engine** (`bevy`, standard view): 0.38 of the asset's pixels are green (g - b above 0.055), most of it the wash; the wash is lighter and yellower in the sun than the flecks.
4. **Differences from the brief**: the wholly green foot blocks read as green lumps, not as stone with moss on it; the brief does not say they should.
