# tree_2

Variant 2 of the broadleaf tree. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

5.5 m wide, 5.4 m deep and 6.2 m tall: lower and wider. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 11. Of seeds 1 to 12 at this size, the generator keeps a tree for 8 (1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11) and refuses the rest with its reasons. Seed 11 was picked from two looked at under Bevy (6, 11): four pads, the widest 3.2 m across and the narrowest 1.9 m, on long limbs that leave the trunk low and wide, which is this variant's character; its first tree is the one kept. It replaces seed 7, which under the first generator sat on the edge of three limits. It measures: fork at 2.3 m (brief 2.0 to 3.5; was 2.1), taper 0.84 (at most 0.9; was 0.87), lean 0.32 m, bark seen 0.038 to 0.060 from all seven views (at least 0.02 from six; was 0.020 from one). The taper is the measure closest to its limit: the generator keeps nothing over 0.85.
