# tree_2

Variant 2 of the broadleaf tree. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/tree/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

5.5 m wide, 5.4 m deep and 6.2 m tall: lower and wider. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the foot of the trunk.

## Seed

Seed 7. Of seeds 1 to 12 at this size, 5 pass every shape check (1, 4, 6, 7, 12); the others fail the gate by check id, most often a fork below 2.0 m, which this lower tree comes close to. Seed 7 was picked from four looked at under Bevy (1, 4, 7, 12): four pads, the crown pad the widest of any variant. A seed draws whole trees until one fills the bounds without being stretched by more than a fifth; the gate, not the generator, refuses a tree that misses the brief.
