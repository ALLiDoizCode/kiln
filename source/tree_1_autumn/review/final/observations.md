# tree_1_autumn, final: observations

Read from `sheet.png` and `benchmarks/out/tree_seasons.png`. The review aids were not made.

1. **Silhouette, proportions, topology, scale**: as `tree_1`; the clay_wire tiles are the same tile for tile (the gate holds the mesh to `tree_1`'s byte for byte).
2. **Materials**: tops of pads golden yellow-orange; the lower edge of each pad orange to red; bark unchanged. In `material_top` the canopy is one yellow-orange with little variation.
3. **In the engine**: `bevy` and `bevy_back`: orange pads with red-orange skirts. `bevy_under` and the views from 3 m and 0.5 m: everything seen from below is dark red-brown, close to the bark's own tone; the limbs separate from the foliage less than in summer, where bark is brown against green.
4. **Differences from the brief**: the brief says "yellow at the top through orange to deep red"; from above it reads orange rather than yellow.
