# slab_2

Variant 2 of the slab. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/slab/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

4.0 m wide, 3.4 m deep and 0.8 m tall: a ledge, hip high, wider than a 3 m foundation of the building grid. The lowest point is at z = 0 and the origin is on the ground under the centre of the bounding box.

## Pieces

Three plates: the dominant one, the taller one pushed into its side, and a small low one at its foot.

## Seed

Seed 2. Of seeds 1 to 40 at this size all 40 build and pass gate L1, with 276 to 312 triangles; only this one was painted and taken through the later gates. Seed 2 was not picked from several. It measures: 312 triangles; 0.173 buried; size steps of 1.92 and 5.73; 0.512 of the view from above level (at least 0.5, the closest to its limit); summit 0.517 off the middle.
