# blade_plant_2

Variant 2 of the blade plant. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/blade_plant/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

1.8 m wide, 1.7 m deep and 1.0 m tall: beside a 1.8 m player it reaches to the hip, and it is as wide as the player is tall. The origin is on the ground at the middle of the rootstock; the rosette reaches a little further one way than the other.
