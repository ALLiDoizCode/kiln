# standing_stone_3

Variant 3 of the standing stone. Everything about it is in the family's brief, `source/standing_stone/brief.md`, except what is here.

## Real-world size

1.0 m wide, 0.9 m deep and 1.8 m tall: a player's own height. The lowest point is at z = 0 and the origin is on the ground under the centre of the bounding box.

## Pieces

Two: the stone and one foot block.

## Seed

Seed 3. Of seeds 1 to 40 at this size all 40 build and pass gate L1, with 136 to 190 triangles; only this one was painted and taken through the later gates. Seed 3 was not picked from several.
