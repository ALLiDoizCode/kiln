# arch_1, final: observations

Sheets opened: `sheet.png`, `bevy_aids.png`, and this variant's rows of `benchmarks/out/arch_variants.png` and `benchmarks/out/arch_1_candidates.png`.

1. **Silhouette.** front, back: two piers and a lintel round one opening; reads in front, back, three_quarter and both bevy tiles. right: the arch is end on and reads as one stepped column, no opening. Rubble: one block at each outer foot, each about 0.25 m tall, a few pixels in front and scale; they read in bevy_back only.
2. **Proportions.** front: the opening is about 0.4 of the width (gate: 1.67 m of 4.2 m) and 0.73 of the height (2.40 of 3.3 m); the lintel is about 0.27 of the height (0.9 m) and 0.85 of the width; the piers' lower blocks are 0.6 of the opening's height, the left pier's upper block is about 0.6 of its lower block's width and the right pier's about 0.35 (0.3 to 0.4 m wide under a 3.6 m lintel).
3. **Facing and grounding.** front and top: the opening runs front to back, the piers stand left and right; the base sits on the bottom edge of the frame in every level view.
4. **Topology.** clay_wire: every piece is planes with a narrow strip round each edge; the lintel's top is cut into 4 triangles; no edge inside a plane except those.
5. **Shading.** material tiles: no face darker than its neighbours without a reason; the joins are dark bands (crevice shadow) where an upper block meets its lower and under the lintel.
6. **Materials.** one grey, lighter at the top (lintel) than at the ground; blotches about a tenth of the lintel's length across on its top and front.
7. **Scale.** scale: the figure is 0.55 of the arch's height (1.8 of 3.3 m) and stands 0.75 of the way up the opening; the opening is about 3 figures' widths wide.
8. **In the engine.** bevy, bevy_back: the lintel's top is the only light surface; the piers' faces toward the camera and the lintel's underside are in the lintel's and their own shadow, and their blotches and edge light barely show. Blender's material tiles show them evenly lit.
9. **Values.** value map of bevy: two masses, the light top and near side of the lintel, and one dark mass of both piers, the lintel's end and the cast shadow, which joins the piers to the ground shadow. The piers do not separate from the shadow behind them.
10. **At a glance.** squint of bevy: "table or gate"; the eye lands on the light lintel top.
11. **Differences from the brief.** The right pier's upper block is slimmer than "stacked blocks" suggests (0.35 of its lower block's width). The upper block of the left pier stands out past the lintel's end and its top shows beside the lintel (front, top). "Nothing upright" does not read: the sides lean 1 to 6 degrees and look upright in front and right. The rubble is small beside the piers (0.25 m against 3.3 m). The lintel is at least 1.1 m deep: top shows about 1.5 m. Seeds 2, 6 and 8 of the candidates sheet have an upper block standing in front of the lintel's face.
